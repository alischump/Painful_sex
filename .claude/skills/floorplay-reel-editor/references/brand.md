# Floorplay Unfiltered brand kit (as used in reels)

Read from Ali's on-brand Canva designs ("Carousel": PELVIC HEALTH / biopsychosocial approach, and the "Did You Know #NN" reel series).

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#FAF8F1` | Card and panel backgrounds (236/255 alpha over video) |
| Olive | `#2D2B1E` | All text, rules, outlines, strike-throughs |
| Yellow | `#F0E958` | Highlighter marks behind key words, caption keywords, handle pill |
| Headline | Instrument Serif, CAPS, tight leading (0.9) | Big statements, numbers ("51%", "3×") |
| Accent | Instrument Serif Italic | Lead-in lines ("why *half* of women with…") |
| Script | Mrs Saint Delafield | Kickers ("occupational therapist", "if this blew your mind") |
| Sans | Inter Medium (labels, tracked about 0.12 em, caps) / SemiBold (captions) | "PELVIC HEALTH" label, body copy, captions |

Motifs:
- **Rule label:** a tracked caps label with a 2 px olive line under it ("PELVIC HEALTH", "A STUDY IN THE JOURNAL OF …")
- **Numbering:** "01." "02." "03." in italic serif
- **Outlined pill:** an olive 3 px outline with an arrow ("SWIPE ⟶" in the carousels, "FOLLOW @floorplayunfiltered ⟶" in reels)
- **Handle tag:** a yellow pill with olive @floorplayunfiltered
- **Cards:** rounded corners, radius about 40 px

Creator name: **Ali**. Role line: "pelvic health occupational therapist".
