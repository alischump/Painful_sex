# Techniques catalogued from reference reels

## Reference A: instagram.com/reel/DVeRdW-E3M9 (talking-head explainer, 85 s)
1. **Hook headline on frame 1**: small serif line with one italic word, then a big caps line.
2. **Jump cuts every 1–3 s**, no breaths left in.
3. **Punch-in zoom** on emphasis lines.
4. **Lowercase captions**, 2–4 words, chest height, small and white.
5. **Keyword pop text** at the top echoing the spoken word ("one thing", "VOICEOVER:", "it's boring.").
6. **Green-screen layout**: B-roll or content fills the top, creator cut out at the bottom. Without B-roll this becomes the split-screen stat panel.
7. **Build-on list**: items appear one by one with small icons ("thoughts · emotion · interests · conversations · reflections").
8. **End list card**: a cream rounded card with a serif title and bullet list over the video, held to the end.
9. Small sticker graphics (coffee cup, mic) popping beside the keyword text. Not used yet; optional.
